# Nexus Product Specification

## Primary Product Positioning
Nexus is a creator-first marketplace, creation suite, orchestration layer, and intelligent workspace for discovering, building, publishing, purchasing, running, and managing AI agents and programmable capabilities.

---

## The 3 Core Loops

### 1. User Loop
$$\text{Discover Agent} \rightarrow \text{Understand Purpose \& Price} \rightarrow \text{Approve Run} \rightarrow \text{Receive Result} \rightarrow \text{Pay Creator} \rightarrow \text{Save \& Reuse}$$

### 2. Creator Loop
$$\text{Describe Agent} \rightarrow \text{Configure Tools \& Permissions} \rightarrow \text{Test} \rightarrow \text{Set Price} \rightarrow \text{Publish} \rightarrow \text{Earn Revenue}$$

### 3. Developer Loop
$$\text{Open Advanced Mode} \rightarrow \text{Inspect Schemas, Providers, Routing, State, Logs, Receipts, \& MCP Tools}$$

---

## What Nexus Is and Is Not

### Nexus Owns:
- Product UX & Command Center
- Unified Marketplace (`/explore`)
- Creator Console & Payouts (`/workspace/creator`)
- Agent & Workflow Creation Suite (`/build`)
- Durable Execution Engine & Receipts (`/activity`)
- Artifact & Knowledge Library (`/library`)
- Tool Permission Disclosures & Safety

### Nexus Is Not:
- A Meridian product or ecosystem portal
- A DeFi application or yield platform
- A custodial wallet or speculative token dashboard
- A collection of disconnected feature demos
- A product requiring users to understand blockchain mechanics
