# NEX Token Utility & Optional Membership Model

## Core Product Positioning
- **USDC remains the primary payment and creator-earnings asset**. All capability listings, provider costs, and creator payouts are dollar-denominated in USDC.
- **MRDN serves strictly as the x402 payment facilitator**. MRDN verifies authorizations and reports settlement status.
- **NEX is an optional ecosystem membership and utility token**. NEX is never required to use Nexus, buy an agent, publish an agent, receive creator earnings, or pay underlying providers.

---

## Membership Lock Tiers (No Passive Yield)
NEX benefits are structured as voluntary membership commitment tiers rather than reward-bearing staking:

| Tier | Optional NEX Commitment | Nexus Fee Discount | Additional Privileges |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Explorer** | 0 NEX | 0% (Standard Fee) | Standard marketplace access, fixed-price agent execution |
| **Builder** | Optional NEX Lock | 25% Fee Discount | Increased concurrent runs, extended result history |
| **Creator** | Optional NEX Lock | 50% Fee Discount | Advanced creator analytics, unlimited agent listings |
| **Studio** | Optional NEX Lock | 75% Fee Discount | Team organization tools, priority execution queues |

---

## Strict Discount Allocation Rules
NEX discounts apply **exclusively to the Nexus marketplace fee** (funded by Nexus).

NEX discounts may **never** reduce:
- Creator net earnings
- Underlying AI model provider costs
- External tool or compute costs
- Storage network costs
- x402 settlement costs owed to facilitators

Example:
```text
Creator price:               $0.65
Provider cost:               $0.25
Standard Nexus fee:          $0.10
NEX membership Nexus fee:    $0.07 (25% discount on $0.10 fee)
Discount funded by Nexus:    $0.03
Total Customer Price:        $0.97
Creator Net Earnings:        $0.65 (Unchanged)
```

---

## Explicitly Disallowed Features
Nexus does **not** implement:
- Fixed APY or passive interest
- Revenue sharing or profit distributions
- Automatic inflationary staking rewards
- Mandatory token purchases for core platform functions
- Production mainnet token contract deployment without legal review
